OgaBiz Data Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 10 October 2026

Requesting deletion of your data

OgaBiz respects your privacy and provides a way to request the deletion of personal information associated with your use of our services.
To request deletion, email ogabizsupport@gmail.com with the subject line OgaBiz Data Deletion Request.
Please include the phone number or email address associated with your OgaBiz account and a brief description of your request. Do not send passwords or other sensitive authentication information.
Verification and processing

We may ask for reasonable information to verify your identity and confirm that you are authorized to make the request.
We will review your request and delete or anonymize eligible personal information in accordance with applicable law and our retention obligations. Some information may need to be retained where legally required or necessary to resolve disputes, prevent fraud, or meet legitimate legal obligations.
We will communicate with you if additional information is needed to process your request.

Contact
For data deletion requests or privacy questions, contact:
OgaBiz Support: ogabizsupport@gmail.com
