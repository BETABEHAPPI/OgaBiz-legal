OgaBiz Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

1. Introduction
OgaBiz (“we,” “our,” or “us”) helps business owners register and manage their business information and access digital business services. This Privacy Policy explains how OgaBiz collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our platform or communicate with us through WhatsApp.

2. Information We May Collect
Depending on the features you use, OgaBiz may collect:
Contact information: phone numbers, email addresses, and names.
Business information: business name, address, business category, and registration details you submit.
WhatsApp communications: messages and information you send to OgaBiz through WhatsApp.
Account and technical information: account identifiers, service activity, and technical information needed to operate and protect the platform.
Please do not send passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information through WhatsApp unless OgaBiz explicitly provides a secure, appropriate process for doing so.

3. How We Use Information
We may use information to:
Register and manage business profiles.
Provide requested OgaBiz features and customer support.
Respond to messages and service requests.
Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the platform.
Detect abuse, protect the service, and comply with applicable legal obligations.
We will use information only for purposes consistent with the service and applicable law.

4. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services
If you interact with OgaBiz through WhatsApp, your messages are processed through Meta's WhatsApp services. Meta may process information under its own privacy policies and terms.
OgaBiz may also use hosting, database, and infrastructure providers to operate the service. Information shared with these providers should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for their service functions.

5. Sharing of Information
We do not intend to sell your personal information. We may disclose information to service providers that help operate OgaBiz, when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or when you direct us to share it.

6. Data Retention and Security
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, meet legitimate operational needs, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.
We use reasonable measures intended to protect information. However, no online service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Requests
You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your use of OgaBiz. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain certain information where legally required.

8. Children's Privacy
OgaBiz is intended for business owners and other users who can lawfully use the service. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

9. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy as OgaBiz evolves. We will update the effective date when we make changes and provide additional notice where required.
